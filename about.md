I'm a recent graduate ('25) from the **University of Texas at Austin** with a B.S. in Mathematics and a B.S. in Computational Biology.

I'm focused on tackling hard interdisciplinary problems and producing high-fidelity computational models of complex biological systems. My work spans the design of eDNA metabarcoding workflows for species identification and mapping, quantifying differential expression between distinct evolutionary developmental stages at the 'omics level, forecasting pharmaceutical logistics for regional healthcare informatics, epidemiological models, and cloud lab operations.

I support **open science** and the FAIR principles: scientific data should be Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable for all. I'm always looking to collaborate on exciting research projects.

*Pronouns: he / him. I loooove learning languages through music, film, travel, and reading.*
